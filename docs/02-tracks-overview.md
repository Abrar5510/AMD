# 02 — Tracks Overview (pick your challenge)

> **Solve a real business problem with AMD technology.** The product must work, and AMD must power a meaningful part of it.

You may enter **one or more** tracks. Your project may use LLMs, computer vision, audio, agents, predictive models, or a mix.

## Compare the tracks

| Track | Partner | One-line goal | Who you're helping | Special rules | Detail |
|-------|---------|---------------|--------------------|---------------|--------|
| 1. Intelligent Industry | Vibe Generation | Help factories spot problems earlier and operate better | Manufacturers, operators, technicians | — | [track-1](tracks/track-1-intelligent-industry.md) |
| 2. Health & Wellbeing | Vibe Generation | Make part of the patient journey safer, clearer or easier | Patients, caregivers, healthcare staff | **No autonomous diagnosis/treatment.** Must show uncertainty + human review | [track-2](tracks/track-2-health-and-wellbeing.md) |

| Evolus: Business Agents | Evolus AI | An agent that runs a real business process end-to-end | Any company's operations team | Must use Evolus workspace + **your own vLLM endpoint on AMD** + ≥2 Evolus building blocks + Evolus APIs | [evolus](tracks/evolus-business-agents.md) |

## Common to every track

- Solve a **real problem for a real user** — say clearly who the user is.
- Show a **working product**, not only a dashboard or presentation.
- Show a **measurable improvement** (time, cost, quality, safety, revenue, workload…).
- **AMD is required** for prize eligibility — see [03-technology-requirements.md](03-technology-requirements.md).

## How to choose (quick guide)

- Have access to **sensor/image/manual data** → Track 1.
- Comfortable with **safety, privacy and human-in-the-loop design** → Track 2.
- Have **sales/inventory/review data** and can build a non-LLM forecast → Track 3.
- Strong on **creative/UX** and can show personalization + unit cost → Track 4.
- Want to automate an **office process** (email, PDF, forms, calls) with an agent → Evolus track (can also combine with a main track).
