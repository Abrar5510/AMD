## AMD

Lead partner. All development happens on cloud AMD GPUs — no hardware to own. Focus: experimenting, benchmarking and running real workloads. Offers AMD Developer Cloud, ROCm, the AMD AI Developer Program, and training resources (see [03-technology-requirements.md](03-technology-requirements.md)).

## Google — Gemma on AMD GPUs

Gemma is Google DeepMind's family of open models; it runs on AMD GPUs via AMD Developer Cloud and ROCm.

## Vibe Generation

Founded by **Paolo Bonetti** to integrate specialized professionals and AI tools into real business processes. Helps companies move from occasional AI use to **hybrid teams and processes** where people and technology work together. Focus: the relationship between AI, talent, judgment and organizational change.

## Evolus AI

A platform to build AI agents that run real business processes. Agents can read documents, answer from company knowledge, run automations, and act on existing systems via APIs and MCP. Also used for "digital employees" in email and Microsoft Teams, and an "AI Switchboard" that answers the phone.

For developers:
- Everything is driven by the **Evolus REST API**
- Agents are reachable through an **OpenAI-compatible endpoint**
- An **MCP server** lets coding agents build on Evolus directly
- During the hackathon, every team **brings its own model**

## PwC Italy

Professional services (tax, legal, audit, consulting). 23 offices in Italy, 9,000+ professionals; part of a global network of 364,000+ people in 136 countries.

## Imment

Helps founders create, fund and manage startups, with expertise in Administration, Finance and Control.

## BAC Network

Runs events, workshops and roundtables to build local entrepreneurial culture; co-invests in innovative startups, provides strategic guidance, and opens its networks to help businesses grow.
