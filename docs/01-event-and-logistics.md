# 01 — Event & Logistics

## What it is

A hands-on hackathon for developers, founders, engineers and builders — any experience level. You build on **AMD GPUs in the cloud** using **ROCm** (AMD's open-source GPU platform, the AMD equivalent of CUDA). Everything runs in the cloud; there is nothing to install on local hardware.

> One hackathon. Three cities. One special weekend in Italy.

## Timeline

| Phase | Dates | Who |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Online | **12–18 October 2026** | Everyone. Open for the whole event. |
| On-site (Italy) | **17–18 October 2026** | Only participants **approved** for in-person attendance. |

- The online hackathon stays open while the on-site weekend happens.
- If you're approved for on-site, you **choose one of the three cities**. On-site gives you hands-on support and a chance to showcase your project live.
