# Track 3 — Reinvent Commerce

**Partner:** Vibe Generation
**Goal:** Help a business understand its customers and take better action.

## The problem

Retailers have product data, transactions, inventory, reviews, support conversations and marketing results — but these sources are **disconnected**, so they rarely turn into decisions.

## What to build

A product that helps a business **attract, serve, convert, or retain** customers.

## Example ideas

- Shopping assistants
- Product discovery and recommendations
- Customer support
- Demand and inventory planning
- Product catalogue enrichment
- Review and sentiment analysis
- Promotion planning
- Churn prevention
- Accessible and multilingual shopping
- In-store computer vision

## ⚠️ Forecasting rule

If your product predicts **demand, sales, or customer behaviour**:

- You must **explain how the prediction was calculated**.
- A language model must **not** be the only forecasting method. Use a real predictive method (statistical model, ML model, etc.); an LLM may explain or wrap it.

## Demo must show

- [ ] Customer or business data turning into a **useful recommendation or action**

## What judges look for

- A real customer or merchant problem
- A useful commercial action
- Relevant recommendations or personalization
- A measurable effect on revenue, margin, conversion, retention, inventory or service quality
