# Track 2 — Health & Wellbeing

**Partner:** Vibe Generation
**Goal:** Make one part of the patient journey safer, clearer, or easier.

## The problem

Patients and healthcare workers struggle with long documents, scattered information, repetitive admin, language barriers and limited time.

## What to build

A product that helps **patients, caregivers, or healthcare staff** handle **one** part of their journey better.

## Example ideas

- Patient intake
- Medical record summaries
- Appointment preparation
- Care navigation
- Clearer discharge instructions
- Multilingual patient communication
- Healthcare document processing
- Administrative workflow automation
- Wellbeing and prevention support

## ⛔ Safety rules (mandatory)

The product **must not** present autonomous diagnosis or treatment as medical advice.

You must **explain how you handle** each of these:

| Risk | Your answer should cover |
|------|--------------------------|
| Incorrect or unsupported responses | How errors are caught / flagged |
| Sensitive information | How health data is protected |
| Uncertain results | How uncertainty is shown to the user |
| High-risk decisions | What the system refuses to decide |
| Human review | When and how a person takes over |

## Demo must show

- [ ] A **realistic case from first input to final result**
- [ ] **Uncertainty made visible** in the output
- [ ] The product **knowing when a person must review or take over**

## What judges look for

- A clearly defined patient or staff need
- Responsible handling of health information
- Useful, understandable results
- A measurable improvement in time, accuracy, accessibility or workload
