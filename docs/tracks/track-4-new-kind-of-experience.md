# Track 4 — Create a New Kind of Experience

**Partner:** Vibe Generation
**Goal:** Build a media, marketing or entertainment experience that wasn't practical before generative AI.

## The bar

Do **more** than generate an image, an ad, or a chatbot. Build something that **responds to its audience, changes with context, or is different for each person**.

## Example ideas

- Personalized video
- Interactive stories
- Audience-driven entertainment
- Adaptive campaigns
- Multilingual creative content
- Generative brand experiences
- Responsive sound or visual environments
- New social media formats
- Accessible media experiences
- Creative quality control

## Demo must show

- [ ] The experience **changing for at least two** different audiences, situations, or inputs

And you must **explain** all five:

| # | Question | Notes |
|---|----------|-------|
| 1 | What did the **team** create? | The human-made parts |
| 2 | What did the **models** generate or decide? | The AI-made parts |
| 3 | How did you control **quality and consistency**? | Guardrails, review, style locks |
| 4 | How much does **each generated experience cost**? | Per-unit cost, ideally in € / $ |
| 5 | **Who would pay** for it, and why? | Business model |

## What judges look for

- An original concept
- A strong user experience
- A thoughtful mix of human creativity and technology
- A product that could work at a **realistic cost**
