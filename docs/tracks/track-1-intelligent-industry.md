# Track 1 — Intelligent Industry

**Partner:** Vibe Generation
**Goal:** Help factories spot problems earlier and operate better.

## The problem

Factories already have plenty of data — machines, cameras, sensors, maintenance reports, technical manuals. The hard part is **knowing what matters and what to do next**.

## What to build

A product that helps a manufacturer do at least one of:

- Reduce **downtime**
- Improve **quality**
- Increase **safety**
- Use **resources** more efficiently

## Example ideas

- Predict equipment failures
- Detect defects from images or video
- Explain production anomalies
- Help technicians search manuals and maintenance records
- Recommend maintenance actions
- Monitor safety conditions
- Reduce energy or material waste

## Demo must show

- [ ] The product processing **realistic industrial data**
- [ ] It producing a useful **alert, explanation, recommendation, or action**
- [ ] **Who** uses it (e.g. operator, technician, plant manager)
- [ ] **What result** it improves

## What judges look for

- A real manufacturing problem
- A working product — **not only a dashboard or presentation**
- Results an operator can understand
- A measurable improvement in time, cost, safety, quality or efficiency
