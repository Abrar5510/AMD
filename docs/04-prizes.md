## Stacking rules

A single project may win:

- the **main challenge prize**, **and/or**
- **one** partner prize, **or both** partner prizes (Google + Evolus).

Example: an Evolus-track agent that uses Gemma served by vLLM on AMD Developer Cloud is eligible for the AMD, Google **and** Evolus prizes.
