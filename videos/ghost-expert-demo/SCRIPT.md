# SCRIPT — ghost-expert-demo

**Voice:** Meredith — Serious & Composed (HeyGen, aa9746e325ff46d482a54fe5bc87890f)
**Voice direction:** Calm, serious, unhurried. A safety briefing, not an ad.

---

## Line 1 — The expert leaves (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0 – 9s
**Delivery:** Quiet and plain.

    Your best technician retires next year. Thirty years of knowing which step gets people hurt. When she leaves, that knowledge leaves with her.

## Line 2 — Film it once (Frame 2)

**Time:** 9 – 24s
**Delivery:** Matter of fact.

    With Ghost Expert, she films the job once and talks through it. Whisper, SigLIP and Gemma cut the video into eight steps, each with a visual check and a safety flag. She fixes what's wrong and approves. The expert owns the procedure. The AI only drafts it.

## Line 3 — The coach (Frame 3)

**Time:** 24 – 35s
**Delivery:** Even.

    Now a new hire does the same job under a camera. Each step turns green as Gemma confirms it, and a voice says what comes next.

## Line 4 — The stop (Frame 4)

**Time:** 35 – 49s
**Delivery:** Firm on "Stop."

    Here is the mistake that hurts technicians: opening the guard before testing that the machine is dead. The screen goes red. Stop. You haven't done step four. Her own hands show how, until the trainee does it.

## Line 5 — It never decides alone (Frame 5)

**Time:** 49 – 68s
**Delivery:** Precise.

    A vision model is sometimes confidently wrong, so it never decides alone. SigLIP watches every frame. Gemma checks two steps in parallel. A deterministic rule engine owns the state, and a stop needs two agreeing checks on different frames. In simulation, with a model that is wrong five percent of the time, false stops on correct trainees fall from fourteen percent to zero.

## Line 6 — Ask, then the record (Frame 6)

**Time:** 68 – 80s
**Delivery:** Lighter.

    Ask out loud where the filter goes, and her clip answers. At the end, the training manager gets a record: time per step, mistakes caught, competent or not.

## Line 7 — One AMD GPU (Frame 7)

**Time:** 80 – 89s
**Delivery:** Proud but brief.

    Four models, Gemma 3, SigLIP, Whisper and Kokoro, run together on one AMD Instinct GPU, served by vLLM on ROCm.

## Line 8 — Film them (Frame 8)

**Time:** 89 – 95s
**Delivery:** Slow. Pause before the last two words.

    Every plant has someone about to retire. Film them.
