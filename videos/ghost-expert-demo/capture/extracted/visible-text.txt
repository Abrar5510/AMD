# Ghost Expert

**Your best technician retires next year, and everything she knows leaves with her. She films one job, once. Ghost Expert watches every new hire do it, coaches them out loud, and stops them before they get hurt.**

Four models on one AMD Instinct GPU: Gemma 3 vision (vLLM on ROCm), SigLIP, Whisper, Kokoro. Full plan: [../docs/13-ghost-expert-build-plan.md](../docs/13-ghost-expert-build-plan.md).

## The 3-minute demo

1. **Author (0:15).** The expert's narrated video goes in. Whisper, SigLIP and Gemma cut it into 8 steps with keyframes, a visual check and a safety flag on each. The expert fixes what's wrong and approves. *The expert owns the procedure; the AI only drafts it.*
2. **Coach (0:35).** A judge does the lockout/tagout job under a top-down camera. Steps turn green as Gemma confirms them, and the voice says what's next.
3. **The stop (1:10).** The judge reaches for the cover screws without testing that the machine is dead. The whole screen goes red, the voice says *"Stop. You haven't done step 4"*, the banner says what the coach saw, and the expert's own hands doing step 4 fill half the screen until the judge does it.
4. **"Where does the filter go?" (1:40).** Hold to ask. The answer is spoken and the expert's clip of that moment plays.
5. **Training record (2:05).** Time per step against the expert, the mistakes caught, a *Competent* or *Needs another run* verdict, and a printable PDF.

If the live take misfires on stage, say "that's why we measure it" and switch to the replay: `/coach?id=loto-filter&src=/files/loto-filter/run.mp4`.

## Why it isn't "just a prompt"

A vision model is sometimes confidently wrong. If one wrong answer can stop a trainee, a correct trainee gets yelled at. So the model never decides alone:

- **SigLIP** (every frame, ~10 ms) only guesses which step this looks like, to decide *when* to ask Gemma.
- **Gemma 3** checks the guessed step and the newest skipped step *in parallel*: one round trip on the stop path.
- **A deterministic rule engine** owns the state. A STOP needs **two agreeing Gemma checks on different frames**. A safety-critical step counts as done only after two agreeing checks, so one wrong "done" can never clear the step the trainee skipped. Unsure → it asks ("can you show me the tag?") instead of accusing. Model down or slow → it stays quiet; it never accuses on a missing answer.

Stress test with a deliberately bad model, where Gemma is confidently wrong 5% of the time and SigLIP is wrong on 25% of frames ([eval/sim_results.md](eval/sim_results.md), `python -m scripts.sim_eval`):

| | Skipped step 4 caught | Correct trainees falsely stopped | Stop latency p50 |
|---|---|---|---|
| One Gemma answer decides | 98% | **14%** | 0.8 s |
| Ghost Expert rule engine | 98% | **0%** | 1.6 s |

That's a simulation of the design, not a measurement of Gemma. The filmed numbers are below.

## If it breaks

- **`rocm/vllm:latest` won't start, or Gemma 3 vision errors:** try a tagged image from [hub.docker.com/r/rocm/vllm/tags](https://hub.docker.com/r/rocm/vllm/tags) via `VLLM_IMAGE` in `.env`. Once one works, pin that tag.
- **`--limit-mm-per-prompt` rejected:** older vLLM wants `image=8` instead of the JSON form. Edit it in `docker-compose.yml`.
- **Permission denied on `/dev/dri`:** add `render` under `group_add` in the `x-amd-gpu` block of `docker-compose.yml`.
- **Out of memory:** lower `MAX_MODEL_LEN` or raise `GPU_MEM_UTIL`.

## Phase 1: the verify call (Gate 1)

The risky core: given 4 frames and a step, Gemma returns `{observed, started, done, confidence}` as strict JSON. Don't build any UI until this passes.

1. **Build the props and film** (plan §2) with the top-down camera you'll demo with. Put the videos in `eval/runs/` (gitignored):
   - `expert1.mp4`: the expert doing all 8 steps in order, narrating.
   - 3 to 5 trainee runs, each with one mistake: skip step 4, skip step 3, steps 5 and 6 swapped.
2. **Label clips.** Watch each video and write down moments: the second of the newest frame, the step, and whether it is started/done at that moment. Copy `eval/verify_labels.example.csv` to `eval/verify_labels.csv`. Aim for at least 40 rows, covering every step in both done and not-done states, and lots of steps 2 and 4.
3. **Optional expert keyframes:** save one frame per step, then set that step's `"keyframe"` to `"keyframes/step2.jpg"` in [procedures/loto-filter/procedure.json](procedures/loto-filter/procedure.json):
   ```bash
   ffmpeg -ss 14.5 -i eval/runs/expert1.mp4 -frames:v 1 procedures/loto-filter/keyframes/step2.jpg
   ```
   Run the eval with and without keyframes and keep whichever scores higher.
4. **Run:**
   ```bash
   docker compose exec app python -m scripts.eval_verify eval/verify_labels.csv
   ```
   Debug one clip with `--video eval/runs/skip4.mp4 --t 41 --step 5`.

**Gate 1:** at least 90% of clips right, and steps 2 and 4 (marked ⚠) right. If it fails, try these in order: bigger and more saturated props, a dark tablecloth, moving the camera closer to the work area, adding keyframes, and `gemma-3-27b-it`. Re-run after each change.

## Phase 2: the coach, replayed (Gate 2)

How it decides ([app/coach.py](app/coach.py)):
- **Fast tier:** SigLIP embeds every frame and compares it with the expert's steps, to guess which step this looks like. It's cheap and can be wrong.
- **Slow tier:** Gemma verifies on a heartbeat (confirm the expected step), plus whenever the fast tier thinks the trainee jumped ahead.
- **Rule engine:** owns the state. A step is done only when Gemma confirms it (twice, on different frames, for a ⚠ step). Before accusing anyone, it re-checks the skipped steps, newest first, because they may be done but not yet confirmed. Skipping a ⚠ step → interrupt; otherwise → hint. Either fires only after `COACH_CONFIRM` (default 2) agreeing checks within `COACH_CONFIRM_S`. If Gemma is unsure, it asks instead of accusing. A failed or slow Gemma call (`VERIFY_TIMEOUT_S`) counts as unsure.

Steps:
1. Copy the expert video to `procedures/loto-filter/expert.mp4` (gitignored). Set each step's `"expert_clip": [start_s, end_s]` in `procedure.json` to the seconds where the expert does it.
2. Build the reference embeddings (commit the resulting `refs.npy`):
   ```bash
   docker compose exec app python -m scripts.build_refs
   ```
   It prints the most similar pair of steps. Close to 1.0 means the fast tier will confuse them: make those steps look more different, or accept that Gemma does the work there.
3. Record trainee runs in `eval/runs/`: at least 5 clean runs and at least 3 that skip step 4. List them in `eval/runs.csv`:
   ```
   video,expect,error_t
   runs/clean1.mp4,,
   runs/skip4-a.mp4,interrupt:4,41.0
   ```
   `error_t` = the second the trainee starts the step they shouldn't (reaching for the cover screws).
4. Replay:
   ```bash
   docker compose exec app python -m scripts.run_eval eval/runs.csv
   ```

**Gate 2:** every skip-4 run is caught and the clean runs have zero false alarms. Tune the `COACH_*` knobs in `.env` (see `.env.example`), re-run, and write down the knobs that pass.

The rule engine has its own check with no GPU or models: `python -m tests.test_coach`. To see how a knob trades catch rate against false alarms before tuning at the venue, run `COACH_CONFIRM=1 python -m scripts.sim_eval`.

## Phase 3: authoring (expert video → procedure)

1. **Draft:** Whisper turns the narration into sentences with word-level timing. SigLIP finds scene changes. Together they cut the video into segments. Gemma sees the narration and numbered contact sheets, one frame per segment, and groups the segments into steps with a title, instruction, visual check, safety flag and common mistake. Code repairs any gaps or overlaps in Gemma's grouping, so steps always cover the whole video in order.
2. **Expert edit:** open `http://<box>:8080/?id=loto-filter`. Fix titles and checks, flip ⚠, move a boundary to the playhead, split, merge or delete steps. Every change is saved and counted ("Expert edits: 2"; that's the Gate 3 number).
3. **Approve:** writes `procedure.json`, keyframes and `refs.npy`, which is everything the coach needs. No separate `build_refs` step is required.

In the browser: pick an id, choose the video, click **Upload and draft** (or pick an existing draft below the form), then select a step to edit it and click **Approve**. **Open coach →** appears once it's approved. From the command line on the box:
```bash
cp my-expert-video.mp4 procedures/loto-filter/expert.mp4
docker compose exec app python -m app.author procedures/loto-filter
docker compose exec app python -m app.author procedures/loto-filter --approve
```

**Narrate while you film.** Short sentences that start with "First / Next / Now / Then / Finally" (or "Poi / Ora / Infine") give the cleanest cuts.

**The app URL is public.** Set `APP_TOKEN` in `.env`: uploads, drafts, edits and approval then need it, and the page picks it up from `?token=...` in the URL. Uploads are capped at `MAX_UPLOAD_MB` (default 500).

**Gate 3 (plan Phase 3):** the LOTO video becomes a correct 8-step procedure after ≤3 edits.

Checks with no models: `python -m tests.test_author`.

**Kokoro on a Mac (local dev only):** the bundled espeak-ng has a broken data path on macOS. Run `brew install espeak-ng` and set `ESPEAK_DATA_PATH=/opt/homebrew/share/espeak-ng-data`. Kokoro also needs Python ≤ 3.12 and `python -m spacy download en_core_web_sm`. The Docker image handles all of this. If espeak is broken, the app logs "Kokoro unavailable" and uses browser speech: espeak exits instead of raising an error, so each language is tested in a child process first and the server stays up.

## Phase 4: the live coach

Open `http://<box>:8080/coach?id=loto-filter&token=<APP_TOKEN>` on the demo laptop, click **Start camera**, and point it top-down at the work area.

- The browser sends 4 frames/s over a WebSocket. The server runs the coach on the newest frame. Frames that arrive while Gemma is thinking are kept for the verify window but not processed, so the session never falls behind.
- **Setup screen** first: the procedure, the coaching language and **Start camera** (or *replay a recording*). Nothing else until the session starts.
- **Step bar** at the top (green done, yellow current, red triangle = safety-critical). The current step's instruction and the **expert keyframe** are on the right. The coach's spoken line shows as a subtitle on the live view.
- **Interrupt:** the whole screen gets a red frame, a STOP banner says which step was skipped and what the coach saw, the voice says it too, and the expert's clip of that step grows to half the screen and loops until the trainee does it.
- **What Gemma sees:** the top-right of the live view shows the model's latest observation ("Gemma · step 4: the hand is near the lamp…"), so the audience can watch it reason.
- **Voice:** Kokoro-82M on the AMD GPU (`af_heart` in English, `if_sara` in Italian). It warms up when the app starts and repeated lines are cached, so an interrupt you've heard before plays instantly. If Kokoro is missing, still warming up, slow (over 3 s) or broken, the page speaks that line with the browser's own voice and the session carries on. The benchmark strip shows `voice Kokoro N ms` or `voice browser`. Set `TTS=browser` in `.env` to skip Kokoro entirely.
- **Benchmark strip** along the bottom, kept quiet: GPU name, VRAM in use (including vLLM), frames/s, SigLIP ms, and Gemma p50/p95.
- **Presenter keys:** `Space` dismisses an interrupt, `N` marks the current step done, `R` resets the session.
- **Replay instead of the camera:** use the file picker, or put a recording at `procedures/<id>/run.mp4` and open `/coach?id=<id>&src=/files/<id>/run.mp4`. This is the stage fallback if the camera or lighting misbehaves.

**Gate 3 (plan Phase 4):** a teammate who hasn't seen the code does the task 3 times. The interrupt fires when they skip step 4, and never when they don't.

## Phase 5: "show me how", bilingual coaching, training record

- **Ask the expert:** hold **Hold to ask** (or hold `Q`) and ask out loud, e.g. "where does the new filter go?". Whisper transcribes the question. Gemma answers in at most two sentences, grounded in the procedure and the expert's narration from `draft.json`, and names the step whose clip shows the answer. The voice speaks the answer and the expert's clip of that step plays once (blue border). The text box next to the button is the fallback for a noisy room.
- **Bilingual:** pick **Italiano** before Start. Gemma translates the step texts once and caches them in `procedure.it.json` until the next approval. Spoken lines come from a small phrase table per language, so they never depend on a live model. Questions are answered in the coaching language.
- **Training record:** opens by itself when the procedure completes, or anytime via **Training record**. It shows the verdict (*Competent* only with zero safety stops and zero presenter overrides), trainee vs expert time per step, the mistakes caught, and the questions asked. **Print / PDF** and **Download JSON** are on the record.

Endpoints: `POST /api/coach/<id>/ask?step=N&lang=en|it` (body: audio, or JSON `{"text": ...}`) and `POST /api/procedures/<id>/translate?lang=it`. Both need `X-App-Token` when `APP_TOKEN` is set.

## 2. The demo task — choose it like a stage prop

The task decides whether the vision model succeeds. Rules: **table-top, top-down camera, big colored parts, order matters, one step that kills people if skipped.**

### Primary: bench-top Lockout/Tagout + filter change ("the machine")

Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) is the real industrial procedure for isolating energy before servicing a machine — consistently among OSHA's most-cited safety standards, and mandatory training in every plant. Skipping "verify zero energy" is the classic fatal error.

**Props (~€40, hardware store):** a lamp or small fan plugged into a switched power strip (= the machine and its isolator) · a **bright red** plug lockout box or padlock · a **yellow** "DO NOT OPERATE" tag · a plastic project box with a cover held by 4 big screws (= the guard) · a colored filter/sponge inside · a screwdriver. Matte dark tablecloth. Phone or USB webcam on a desk arm, top-down.

| # | Step | Visual check (what the model looks for) | Safety-critical | Must come after |
|---|------|-----------------------------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Switch the machine off at the strip | strip switch light off, lamp off | — | — |
| 2 | Unplug and lock the plug in the red box | plug inside red lockout box, closed | ✅ | 1 |
| 3 | Hang the yellow tag | yellow tag attached to box | ✅ | 2 |
| 4 | **Verify zero energy: press the machine's own switch** | hand presses lamp switch, lamp stays off | ✅ | 3 |
| 5 | Remove the 4 cover screws, lift the cover | cover off, screws on tray | ✅ | 4 |
| 6 | Swap the filter (old out, new in) | new filter color visible inside | — | 5 |
| 7 | Cover on, 4 screws back | cover on, tray empty | — | 6 |
| 8 | Remove tag + lock, plug in, switch on | lamp on | — | 7 |

**The planted mistake:** a judge removes the cover (step 5) without doing step 4. That is the interrupt.

### Backup task: LEGO build of a small "gearbox" (8 steps, colored bricks)

Zero hardware risk, everybody understands it. Use it as a fallback, or as the "second procedure" that proves authoring is general: *"We taught it this one in 90 seconds, here."*

## 3. Architecture

```
 BROWSER (demo laptop)                       AMD INSTINCT GPU SERVER (one box, docker compose)
 ┌──────────────────────────┐   WebSocket   ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ getUserMedia camera+mic  │──JPEG 4 fps──►│ FastAPI app                                       │
 │ Coach UI / Author UI     │──audio (PTT)─►│  ├─ fast tier: SigLIP embeddings → step tracker   │
 │ plays TTS audio + clips  │◄─events/audio─│  ├─ rule engine (deterministic state machine)     │
 └──────────────────────────┘               │  ├─ slow tier: Gemma 3 vision verify (via vLLM)   │
                                            │  ├─ Whisper (ASR)    ├─ Kokoro (TTS)              │
                                            │  └─ procedures/ (JSON + keyframes + expert mp4)   │
                                            │ vLLM server: Gemma 3 12B/27B-it, OpenAI API       │
                                            └───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**The key design decision:** the LLM never decides alone. Three layers:

1. **Fast tier (every frame, ~10 ms):** SigLIP image embedding vs each step's reference embeddings from the expert video → smoothed "which step does this look like" scores. Cheap, always on, can be wrong.
2. **Slow tier (on trigger, ~0.5–1 s):** Gemma 3 gets the last 4 frames + the expert keyframe + that step's visual check → strict JSON verdict. Triggered when the fast tier sees a step change, plus a heartbeat every ~2 s.
3. **Rule engine (deterministic Python):** owns the state. A step is *done* only when Gemma confirms it. If the trainee is starting step *k* while a `must_come_after` step is not confirmed **and** *k* is safety-critical → **INTERRUPT**. Non-critical → gentle hint. This is the "not just a prompt" answer for the judges.

## 4. Models and algorithms

### 4.1 Model lineup (all on one GPU)

| Job | Model | Why | Fits? |
|-----|-------|-----|-------|
| See + reason (verify steps, write procedure, answer questions) | **Gemma 3 12B-it** (or 27B-it if latency allows) via **vLLM on ROCm** | vision + multilingual + structured JSON; unlocks the Google prize | 27B in bf16 ≈ 55 GB |
| Fast frame embeddings | **SigLIP so400m** (PyTorch) | same family as Gemma 3's vision encoder; ~10 ms/frame | ~2 GB |
| Speech to text (expert narration, trainee questions) | **Whisper large-v3-turbo** (HF transformers on ROCm) | timestamps for authoring; robust Italian/English | ~2 GB |
| Text to speech (coach voice) | **Kokoro-82M** | tiny, fast, natural | <1 GB |

Everything fits on one MI300X-class GPU with room to spare — that is a slide: *"four models, one GPU, no swapping."* Pick 12B vs 27B by the eval (§5.4), not by vibes.

### 4.2 Authoring pipeline (`author.py`) — expert video → procedure

1. `ffmpeg`: frames at 2 fps (512 px) + audio track.
2. Whisper → transcript with word timestamps.
3. SigLIP embedding per frame → **change points** (cosine distance between rolling windows spikes) → candidate step boundaries. Merge with transcript cues ("next", "now", "then", pauses > 1.5 s).
4. Gemma 3 gets: transcript + a contact sheet of one keyframe per candidate segment → returns the procedure with **structured outputs (JSON schema)** so it can't return junk:

```json
{
  "id": "loto-filter",
  "title": "Lockout/tagout + filter change",
  "steps": [
    {
      "id": 4,
      "title": "Verify zero energy",
      "instruction": "Press the machine's own switch. It must stay off.",
      "visual_check": "a hand presses the lamp's switch and the lamp stays off",
      "safety_critical": true,
      "must_come_after": [3],
      "common_mistake": "Opening the cover without testing the switch",
      "expert_clip": [41.5, 47.0],
      "keyframe": "keyframes/step4.jpg"
    }
  ]
}
```

5. **Expert review screen:** the expert edits titles, flips `safety_critical`, fixes boundaries by dragging. Approve → `refs.npy` (mean SigLIP embedding of each step's frames) is saved. This human gate is part of the product: *the expert owns the procedure, the AI drafts it.*

**Bonus output for free:** the approved procedure renders as a printable work instruction (steps + keyframes). Writing those by hand takes hours — mention it, it's real business value.

### 4.3 Live coaching loop (`coach.py`)

```python
# per frame (fast tier)
emb = siglip(frame)
scores = ema(cosine(emb, refs))          # one score per step, smoothed
guess = argmax(scores)

# rule engine — owns the truth
if guess != state.current and scores[guess] > ENTER_T:     # looks like a new step
    verdict = gemma_verify(last_frames(4), step=guess)     # slow tier
    blockers = [s for s in steps[guess].must_come_after if s not in state.done]
    if steps[guess].safety_critical and blockers and verdict.started:
        interrupt(blockers[0])     # voice + red banner + expert clip of the skipped step
    elif verdict.done and verdict.confidence > DONE_T:
        state.done.add(guess)
every ~2 s: gemma_verify(last_frames(4), step=state.current)   # heartbeat confirms completions
```

- **Two-confirmation rule:** interrupt only when the fast tier *and* Gemma agree the trainee is in the forbidden step. False alarms are worse than a half-second delay.
- **Ask, don't accuse, when unsure:** if confidence is in the grey zone → *"Can you show me the tag?"* instead of an alarm. That is the visible uncertainty.
- **Calibration knobs** in `.env`: `ENTER_T`, `DONE_T`, `INTERRUPT_CONFIRMATIONS`, frame rate. Tune them on the eval set, at the venue.

### 4.4 The verify prompt (`prompts.py`) — short, strict, cached

System prompt + procedure + expert keyframes are **identical across calls** → vLLM's automatic prefix caching makes every verify call only pay for the 4 new frames. Order the prompt so the constant part comes first.

```
You are checking one step of an industrial procedure from a top-down camera.
Step: {title}. It is done when: {visual_check}.
Image 1 is the expert doing it. Images 2–5 are the trainee, oldest to newest.
Return JSON: {"started": bool, "done": bool, "confidence": 0-1,
              "observed": "<one short sentence of what you see>"}
If you cannot see the relevant parts, set confidence below 0.4.
```

Output is a handful of tokens → latency is dominated by image prefill (Gemma 3 uses 256 tokens per image), which is exactly where the big GPU helps.

### 4.5 "Show me how" (voice Q&A)

Push-to-talk → Whisper → Gemma with {procedure, current step, transcript slice of the expert} → short answer → Kokoro voice **+ the app jumps the expert video to `expert_clip` of the relevant step**. The moment: the trainee asks *"where does the filter go?"* and the expert's own hands answer.

### 4.6 UI spec (`web/`) — one page, three views

- **Coach view (the stage):** left = live camera; right = expert keyframe of the current step; top = step rail (✓ done · ● current · ○ to do; safety steps marked ⚠); bottom = coach caption (what it just said) + confidence chip. Interrupt = whole frame goes red, expert clip of the skipped step plays.
- **Author view:** expert video with a timeline of detected steps; editable step cards; Approve.
- **Report view:** time per step vs expert, mistakes caught, hints given, "competent / needs another run" — the training manager's screen.
- **Benchmark panel (always visible, small):** GPU name, VRAM used, frames/s, verify latency p50/p95, tokens/s.

## 6. Build order (dependency-ordered, no clock)

Advance only when the gate passes. Every phase leaves something demo-able behind.

### Phase 0 — Serving + props + expert video
§1 checklist. **Gate:** Gemma 3 answers a 4-image request on your AMD box; you know the 12B and 27B latency; the props exist; `expert.mp4` is recorded.

### Phase 1 — The verify call (the risky core)
`prompts.py` + a CLI: take 4 frames from a recording + a step → JSON verdict. Run it on frames you labeled by hand.
**GATE 1: the model correctly says done/not-done on ≥90% of hand-labeled step clips, especially step 4 (switch press) and step 2 (plug in box).** If it fails: make the props bigger and more colorful, raise the resolution, crop to the work area, add a second keyframe. **Do not build UI before this passes.**

### Phase 2 — Fast tier + rule engine, offline
`models.py` (SigLIP) + `coach.py`, run on recorded runs (no camera yet).
**GATE 2: `run_eval.py` catches the skip-step-4 error in recorded runs with zero false alarms on 5 clean runs.**

### Phase 3 — Authoring
`author.py` → procedure JSON → expert edit view. It's fine if the first draft needs edits — the edit screen is the feature.
**Gate:** the LOTO video becomes a correct 8-step procedure after ≤3 edits.

### Phase 4 — Live app
WebSocket camera stream, coach view, TTS, interrupt with expert clip. Run the app on the AMD box, open the port or tunnel, so the submission URL is live.
**GATE 3: a teammate who hasn't seen the code does the task 3 times; the interrupt fires when they skip step 4, and never when they don't.**

### Phase 5 — "Show me how", report, bilingual
Small pieces on top of what exists. Drop any of them freely.

### Phase 6 — Eval + benchmarks + film
Record the 20–30 runs, run the eval, capture benchmarks, film the §5.2 script.

### Phase 7 — README, slides, submission
README with the eval table and a `docker compose up` that works. Cover image = the red interrupt frame. **After submitting, only polish the video and slides.**

### Cut list — drop in this order
1. Concurrent streams benchmark (use one measured number). 2. Bilingual. 3. Report view (show it in a slide). 4. Voice Q&A (keep the clip replay on a button). 5. Authoring UI (edit the JSON by hand, show the pipeline output in the video). 6. Live stage demo — the video carries the entry.

## 8. What could break (risk register)

| # | Risk | You'll notice it when… | Mitigation | Fallback |
|---|------|------------------------|------------|----------|
| 1 | **vLLM + Gemma 3 vision won't run on the ROCm image** | Phase 0 smoke test fails | try the official ROCm vLLM image + a different vLLM version; serve with HF transformers as a slower backup | Qwen2.5-VL on vLLM (works, but loses the Google prize angle) |
| 2 | **Verify too slow (>1.5 s)** → interrupt arrives after the cover is off | latency p95 in Phase 2 | 12B instead of 27B, 3 frames not 4, 384 px crops of the work area, prefix caching, trigger earlier (when the hand approaches the screws) | interrupt on fast-tier + rule engine alone for the one critical transition |
| 3 | **False alarms** — coach nags a correct trainee | clean eval runs fire interrupts | two-confirmation rule, higher thresholds, "ask, don't accuse" grey zone | narrow interrupts to step 4 only, hints elsewhere |
| 4 | **Model can't see small things** (tag on, plug in box, lamp on/off) | Gate 1 fails on those steps | big saturated props, top-down fixed camera, dark cloth, crop to the work area, include the expert keyframe | rework the step so it's visible (e.g., a big lamp the model can't miss) |
| 5 | **Venue looks different from the expert video** (light, table, sleeves) | fast-tier scores flat or wrong | record the expert video at the venue; refs re-record in 3 minutes; Gemma is the authority, SigLIP only triggers | heartbeat-only mode (Gemma every 1.5 s, no fast tier) |
| 6 | **Network jitter** laptop ↔ AMD cloud | frames arrive in bursts, latency spikes | 4 fps small JPEGs, drop stale frames server-side, phone hotspot as backup network | play the recorded perfect run |
| 7 | **Authoring segments the video wrong** | wrong step count | narrate clearly ("step four…") — transcript cues dominate; expert edit screen | hand-written procedure.json (still show the pipeline in the video) |
| 8 | **Audio chaos on stage** (coach voice + room noise) | Whisper mishears | push-to-talk, a cheap lapel mic for the judge, captions on screen | typed question box |
| 9 | **Judge does something nobody predicted** | step tracker gets lost | "I lost you — which step are you on?" fallback line; manual step-advance key for the presenter | presenter hotkey to reset the session |
| 10 | **GPU instance unavailable on demo day** | instance won't start | keep the instance alive, docker image pushed, a second region/account noted | recorded video + eval numbers carry the entry |
